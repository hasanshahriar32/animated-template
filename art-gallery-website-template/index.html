Art after dark

Lights down at nine. Eyes open till two.

Nocturna is a kunsthalle that keeps night hours. Four rooms, a projection hall and a walled garden open when the city's museums close, and stay open while you still want to look.

Before you come

Questions for the night

Anything else: ask at the door, or write to us before the evening.

Is it really open until two?

Yes, Thursday to Sunday. Last entry is at 01:30; the rooms close at 02:00 and the garden bar ten minutes earlier.

Do I need a ticket for the Friday screening?

Seats are free with the Night Pass and €9 at the door. The hall holds ninety people; pass holders can reserve until Thursday noon.

Is the building accessible?

All four rooms and the hall are step-free, with a lift to the first floor. The light stays low, so ask at the door for a hand torch.

Can I bring children?

Before 22:00, with an adult. After that the rooms are for visitors aged sixteen and over.

Can I take photographs?

Yes, without flash. The Red Room asks for no photographs while its installation is running.
