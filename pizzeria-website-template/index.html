Pizza for the Whole block

Since 1994 on the same corner, with the same oven and the same noisy tables. Some things never go out of fashion because they were never a fashion.

As you'll see, I'm not very fancy. I'm more of a shared-table, extra-napkins and one-more-slice kind of place.

Thin in the middle, puffed at the edge, eaten with hands. The neighbours have been squeezing round my tables for thirty years, and I like it loud.

Meet my people

I take my time

Every pie you eat here started two days before you sat down. Five steps, no shortcuts, the same since my first night on Example Street.

  1. Step 1

    Rest

    Forty-eight hours in the cold. The dough does the work, I just wait.

  2. Step 2

    Knead

    By hand, at seven in the morning, before the street wakes up.

  3. Step 3

    Stretch

    Fingers from the middle out. A rolling pin never touches it.

  4. Step 4

    Sauce

    Plum tomatoes, salt and one spiral of it. Nothing else.

  5. Step 5

    Fire

    Ninety seconds in the wood oven. Puffed, spotted, done.

Asked at the counter

The things the neighbours ask me most. Anything else, shout through the hatch.

Can I book for a big group?

Up to twenty-four of you on the long table, any night but Thursday. Book through the form below or call the shop.

Is there anything gluten-free?

Yes: a gluten-free base for any pie, baked on its own tray. Tell us about any allergy when you order.

Are dogs welcome?

Very. Water bowl by the door, crusts by negotiation.

Do you deliver?

Within a mile of Example Street on our own bike, from five o'clock every night we're open.

Can I take dough home?

Dough balls are £2 each at the slice window, rested and ready to stretch. Bake them as hot as your oven goes.

What brings you here?

Tell me what you need and we'll sort it, love. We answer the same day, usually between two pies.
