Halyard Ahead · four live evenings · from 21 October 2026

Attention doesn’t drift. It turns.

Every year we sit with thousands of people and watch what they actually choose to look at — not what they say they like. The 2027 Forecast maps six turns in that attention, and where a brand should be standing when it arrives.

3,200 conversations · nine markets · 41,000 hours of viewing diaries

Six signals for 2027

Read
the
bearing

Each signal carries a bearing: how steeply it is rising or cooling across our nine markets. The arrow takes the bearing of the signal in front of you.

  1. Rising · 8 of 9 markets

    Smaller rooms

    Audiences are leaving the open feed for group chats, close-friends lists and paid circles. Influence now travels through rooms of thirty, not crowds of millions.

  2. Rising · 7 of 9 markets

    Slow proof

    The review filmed a year after purchase now outperforms the unboxing. People trust what has survived real life — and they are willing to wait for it.

  3. Rising · 6 of 9 markets

    The unpolished premium

    Lo-fi is no longer the cheap option. Kept mistakes and one-take answers read as respect for the viewer’s time; heavy polish now reads as distance.

  4. Cooling · 7 of 9 markets

    Borrowed authority

    Generalists who endorse everything are losing pull. People now ask who has spent ten years on one narrow thing, and they listen to that person instead.

  5. Rising fast · 9 of 9 markets

    Questions go out loud

    What used to be typed into a search bar is now asked in comment threads, voice notes and assistants. Brands need answers that people can quote back.

  6. Cooling · 8 of 9 markets

    Reach without recall

    Impressions are up and memory is down. Media bought without a reason to remember is, in one respondent’s words, paying for weather.

What brands count, and what people act on

Six measures still head most reports. Our respondents act on something else. Here is the trade, one line at a time.

  1. From Follower count

    to Reply rate

    A reply is a promise somebody kept. In our panel it predicted purchase twice as well as audience size.

  2. From Launch day

    to Day 400

    The second wave of reviews, filmed after a year of use, now moves more people than the launch itself.

  3. From Celebrity

    to Specialist

    Fame still opens the door. A decade spent on one narrow subject is what gets people to walk through it.

  4. From Feed

    to Room

    Things are discovered in public and decided in private. The group chat is where the verdict happens.

  5. From Script

    to Draft

    Answers that look unrehearsed beat finished ones, as long as the facts hold up.

  6. From Reach

    to Recall

    We now ask one question before any media plan: what will someone repeat to a friend tomorrow?

Before you ask

Five
straight
answers

Is the forecast really free?

Yes. Leave a work email and the PDF arrives within a minute. We write once more when the next edition is out, and that is all.

Can I attend a session in person?

Each evening has sixty seats in our Bristol studio, given out in the order requests arrive. Everyone else watches the free broadcast.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Yes. Recordings go up within two days, with chapters, so you can jump straight to the argument you care about.

How is the research done?

A panel of 3,200 people in nine markets, interviewed twice a year, plus 41,000 hours of viewing diaries and screen recordings people chose to share. The method chapter lists every source.

Can you present it to my team?

We run a ninety-minute working session for up to forty people, in person or online, built around the two signals that matter most to your brand.

Point us somewhere

Briefs, invitations and arguments with the forecast are all welcome. We answer within two working days.
