Built in Bristol from fewer parts.

A petrol moped has around two thousand parts. An Arco has two hundred and forty. No oil, no chain, no spark plugs: the motor sits sealed inside the rear wheel.

Frames are welded and painted at our Harbourside works, then every moped rides a test loop of the docks before it leaves.

Fewer parts means fewer things to wear out. A yearly service is mostly tyres, brakes, a software check and a cup of tea.

Before you ride.

Which licence do I need?

A full car licence lets you ride any Arco. Without one, you need a CBT certificate from the age of sixteen; we can book the course for you.

How far does it really go in winter?

Cold batteries give up about a fifth of their range, so plan on sixty kilometres on a frosty week. Charging indoors keeps the cells warm and helps.

Can I charge it in a flat?

Yes. The battery lifts out and plugs into any ordinary socket with the charger from the box. A spare battery doubles your range if you have nowhere to park near a socket.

Is it all right in the rain?

The motor and battery are sealed, so a Bristol downpour is no trouble. Riding through deep standing water is the one thing we ask you to avoid.

What if it is stolen?

Every Arco has a tracker and an alarm in the app, and it will not start without your phone or key card nearby. Monthly riders get a replacement within a day.
