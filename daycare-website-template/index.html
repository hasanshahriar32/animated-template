Settling in, one step at a time

Two weeks, four small steps. You stay as long as your child needs you, and step back only when Jess and your child are ready.

  1. Days 1 and 2

    Stay and play together

    You come in with your child for an hour. Jess plays alongside you both and learns the songs, the snacks and the words for everything.

  2. Days 3 and 4

    A short goodbye

    You say goodbye at the door and wait in the kitchen with a cup of tea. Twenty minutes, then back for a cuddle.

  3. Days 5 to 7

    A morning on their own

    A whole morning with Jess and the room, lunch included. We send photos so you can see how it's going.

  4. Week two

    A full day, then home-time hugs

    The first full day, with Jess close by. Most children have a favourite friend by the end of the week.

Questions parents ask

And if yours isn't here, ask us at an open morning or call the house: someone always picks up.

What ages do you take?

From twelve months until children start school, usually the September after they turn four.

Do you take funded hours?

Yes, 15 and 30 funded hours for every age we care for, term-time or stretched across the year. We take them off your bill before we send it.

What should my child bring?

Wellies, a spare set of clothes and a comforter if they have one. We provide nappies, wipes, sun cream and every meal and snack.

How do you handle allergies?

Our kitchen is nut-free. Rosa sits down with you before the first day and plans every allergy and diet, and each room has the list on the wall.

Is there a waiting list?

For Buds there often is. Register at an open morning and we will tell you honestly how long it is likely to be.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 7:30 to 18:00, 51 weeks a year. We close between Christmas and New Year.
