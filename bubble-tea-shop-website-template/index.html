How to order

Three steps, about three minutes, no app needed.

  1. Step 1

    Pick

    Choose a signature cup or build your own on the counter screen. Tasters of any tea are free.

  2. Step 2

    Shake

    We brew, pour and shake it in front of you, ten seconds by hand, then seal the lid.

  3. Step 3

    Sip

    Stab the lid with the fat straw, give it a swirl and get a pearl in every sip.

House rules

  • Tea, not powder

    Every base is brewed from loose leaf in small pots, and a pot that turns two hours old is poured away.

  • Pearls die young

    Cooked in small batches through the day and binned at two hours, cooked or not. No exceptions on a busy Saturday.

  • Your sweetness, your call

    We will tell you how we like it, then make it exactly how you asked. Zero sugar is a real option here.

  • Bring the cup back

    Any clean cup up to 700 ml gets 50p off, and our own glass cups come with a free first refill.

Good questions

Anything else, ask at the counter or message us. We answer within the hour while we are open.

Is anything vegan?

Yes. Every tea, oat milk, all fruit bases and our brown-sugar pearls are vegan. Only fresh milk, cheese foam and taro paste are not.

How much caffeine is in a cup?

A regular jasmine or Assam cup has about as much as a small coffee. Butterfly pea and every fruit base have none.

Where can I see allergens?

The full allergen sheet sits on the counter and on the order screen. Popping pearls contain seaweed extract; taro paste contains milk.

Can I order ahead?

Call us and we will have it shaken as you walk in. For ten cups or more, use the group order form below.

Can I bring my own cup?

Please do. Any clean cup up to 700 ml gets 50p off, hot or cold.
