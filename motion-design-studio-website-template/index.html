Every brand gets a tempo

Before we animate anything, we choose how your brand moves: how fast it arrives, how hard it stops, how long it holds. Three of the tempos we have written so far:

  • Glide

    A long ease-out. Nothing arrives in a hurry; for brands that should feel inevitable.

  • Snap

    Fast in, hard stop, a beat of stillness. For brands that make decisions.

  • Pulse

    An even swing with a heartbeat on top. For brands that live with their audience every day.

From first call to final frame

Four steps, about six weeks for a launch film. The tempo is set in step one and every frame after it is checked against it.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Two workshops with your team. We leave with the tempo of your brand written down in one page.

  2. 02

    Frame

    Storyboards and motion tests: the key moves animated rough, before anything is polished.

  3. 03

    Move

    Shoot, animate and edit in one room, with weekly reviews you can join from anywhere.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Masters, cutdowns for every channel and the motion rules, handed to your team.

The lab, after hours

What we shoot when nobody has asked for anything. Half of our best client ideas started here as a test on a Friday night.

  • Afterimage

    A tunnel drawn with one torch and forty seconds of shutter.

  • One line

    How little can move and still read as speed.

  • Ring study

    A loop with no start, shot in one take.

  • Lightwalker

    A person sketched in the air, then erased by the next frame.

  • Heat

    A camera swung past a lamp until only the colour stayed.

  • Red shift

    Tail lights at a hundred and twenty, slowed to a crawl.

Before you call

Short answers to what new clients ask first. Anything else, write to us.

What is a motion system?

A short set of rules for how your brand moves: easing curves, timing, transitions, how type and logo enter and leave. It comes as a document, project files and sample animations your team or any agency can use.

Do you work with brands outside the Netherlands?

Most of our clients are elsewhere in Europe and in North America. Workshops run in person or remotely; reviews are always remote.

Can you work with our existing agency?

Yes. We often write the motion system and the launch film, then hand the rules to the agency that runs your campaigns.

Who owns the files?

You do. Masters, project files and the motion rules are yours from the final invoice on.

How soon can you start?

A sprint usually starts within three weeks of the first call, a season within six.
