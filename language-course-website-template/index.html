Wait, how do you say that?

A language school for curious grown-ups. Six languages, evening groups of eight, and lessons built from the conversations you actually have.

Book a free trial See the courses

Every big conversation starts with small talk. The weather, the train, the coffee, the barber. We teach the ordinary, so the extraordinary has somewhere to go.

  • ¿Qué tal?
  • Lovely weather, huh?
  • Ça va ?
  • Come va?
  • Tudo bem?
  • Wie geht's?
  • Is this seat taken?
  • O-genki desu ka?

The syllabus is your Tuesday

Twelve weeks, twelve ordinary situations. You learn what to say at the barber long before anyone mentions the subjunctive.

  1. Week 1

    Ordering coffee

    “Un cortado, por favor.”

  2. Week 2

    The train is late

    “C’est bien le quai pour Lyon ?”

  3. Week 3

    Haggling at the market

    “Quanto costa questo?”

  4. Week 4

    Small talk at the barber

    “Só um pouco nas pontas.”

  5. Week 5

    Dinner with strangers

    “Was empfehlen Sie?”

  6. Week 6

    Asking the way

    “Sumimasen, eki wa doko desu ka?”

Then the doctor, the landlord, a first date, a job interview, a polite complaint and a wedding toast. Pick your language

Not sure where to start?

Three small questions. Answer honestly; nobody is listening. Or skip them and come to a free trial: we place everyone in ten minutes.

A stranger at the bus stop says hello in the language you want to learn. You…
A waiter rattles off today’s specials. You…
Someone tells a joke in that language. You…

Answer all three and we will suggest a group.

Start at A1, the beginners’ group: Tuesdays at 6:30 pm.

Start at A2, the improvers: Thursdays at 6:30 pm.

Start at B2, the conversation group: Saturdays at 10 am.

Fees with no small print

The small print, at full size

Books and audio are included. Miss a class and join another group that week. Pay in three instalments at no extra cost. Cancel before week two for a full refund. That is all of it.
