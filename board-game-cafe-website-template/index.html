Table guides

We read the rulebook so you don't have to

There is a guide in every room. They have played everything on the shelves at least twice, and teaching you is part of the table fee.

  1. 01

    Tell us who's playing

    How many of you, how long you've got, and whether anyone has played anything heavier than snap.

  2. 02

    Pick from three

    Your guide brings three boxes that fit your table. Nobody has to browse seven hundred spines.

  3. 03

    Play the first round together

    They set up, explain the goal in a minute and play the opening turns with you until it clicks.

  4. 04

    Raise the flag card

    Every table has one. Stand it up when a rule starts an argument and a guide comes over to settle it.

Questions

Before you come

Anything else, ring the café on 0114 000 0000.

Do we need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome on weekdays. Book for Friday and Saturday evenings, and for groups of six or more.

How long can we stay?

As long as you like: the table fee covers the whole day. On busy evenings we ask big groups to keep to four hours.

Can you teach us a game we've never played?

That is what the guides are for, and it is included in the table fee. Ask as often as you like.

Is it all right to bring children?

Yes. Under-12s pay £3, Saturday mornings are set aside for families and the first-timers shelf is full of games for seven-year-olds.

Can we bring our own game?

Of course. The table fee is the same, and a guide will help you set it up.

Do you cater for allergies?

Tell us when you order. The kitchen keeps gluten-free bread and vegan cheese, and the full allergen list is at the counter.

Is the café step-free?

The ground floor, the toilets and the long table room are step-free. The quiet room upstairs is reached by stairs.
