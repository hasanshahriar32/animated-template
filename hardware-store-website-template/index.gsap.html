Len Hollis

Third owner, behind the counter since 2004

Len bought Sixpenny from the Ashworths, who had it from the man who named it after the two-inch nail he sold most of in 1968.

Nothing has been moved much since. The drawers still run from small to large, and someone who knows what is in them is always on the floor.

“I would rather sell you one screw that fits than a box of fifty that don’t.”

Asked at the counter

Do you really sell screws one at a time?

Yes. Every drawer in aisle 2 is priced by the one, by the ten and by the box, so you buy what the job needs and not a tin of spares for the shed.

Can you match a paint colour from a chip?

Bring a flake the size of a fingernail. The scanner reads it and we mix it in about ten minutes, in any finish from flat matt to full gloss.

Do you deliver?

Within three miles on Tuesdays and Fridays. It costs £5 and is free over £60, timber and sheet materials included.

How does tool hire work?

Reserve by phone, collect from three on Friday and return by ten on Monday. We take a refundable deposit and photo ID, and show you how the tool works before it leaves.

Can I bring things back?

Unused and in its packet, within 30 days, with the receipt. Cut keys, mixed paint and cut timber are made for you, so they stay with you.

Which keys can you cut?

Most house, shed and padlock keys while you wait. Security and car keys are ordered in and take three to five days.
