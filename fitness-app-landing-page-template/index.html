Questions,
answered

Still unsure? The answers below cover most of what people ask before they start.

Ask us something else
Do I need a smartwatch?

No. A watch or ring makes the recovery score sharper, but three taps each morning about sleep and soreness are enough to bend the plan.

How does the plan adapt?

Every morning Kurvo compares your recovery, your calendar and the week's goal, then moves, shortens or swaps sessions. You see every change and can undo it.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Tell the app what equipment you have, even if it is nothing, and every session is written for it.

What if I miss a session?

Nothing breaks. The week re-balances so the important work still happens, without stacking two hard days together.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, in two taps from the app's account page. You keep access until the end of the paid month.
