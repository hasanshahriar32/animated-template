Layers, not coats

A coat of polish sits on top of the nail. A layer of gel becomes part of it: brushed thin, cured for sixty seconds, then the next. Six to nine of them make a set that stays glassy for three weeks and comes off without taking the nail with it.

Built in four layers

A set takes fifty to eighty minutes. Most of it is waiting for the lamp, and we never rush the lamp.

  1. Layer 1

    Prep

    Cuticles pushed back, never cut. The surface buffed just enough for gel to hold.

  2. Layer 2

    Base

    A thin structured base levels each nail and carries its shape.

  3. Layer 3

    Colour

    Two or three sheer passes, each cured for sixty seconds before the next.

  4. Layer 4

    Seal

    A clear top layer cured twice and buffed to a glass edge. Nothing sticky left.

Your file is yours

Your first visit ends with a glass file etched with your initials. It waits in a drawer here between visits, or goes home with you. Never shared, never thrown away.

  1. 1

    Oil at night

    A drop of cuticle oil before bed keeps the edges sealed and stops them lifting.

  2. 2

    File one way

    Short strokes in one direction with your own glass file. Sawing back and forth splits the edge.

  3. 3

    Gloves at the sink

    Hot water and washing-up liquid soften gel faster than anything else you will do all week.

  4. 4

    Back at three weeks

    Refill before the gap at the cuticle reaches a millimetre and a half, and the set stays level.

Before you come

Anything else, ask at the desk or write to us. We answer the same day.

How long does a set last?

Three weeks on most nails, up to four for a bare builder set. Refill before the growth gap reaches a millimetre and a half.

Will gel damage my nails?

Careless removal does, not the gel. We soak and lift, never drill into your own nail, and Ines runs bare sets for nails that need a rest.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes. Add a photo to your booking note so we can set aside time for the painting and pick the right artist.

What if a nail chips?

Come back within seven days and we repair it free, no questions asked.

Do you take walk-ins?

When a table is free. Ring ahead and we will tell you honestly how long the wait is.
