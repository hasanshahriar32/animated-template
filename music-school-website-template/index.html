From the first lesson to the first stage

Most students play their first short set within two terms. This is the way there, one step a season.

  1. Step 1

    Free trial lesson

    Thirty minutes to meet a mentor, play a little and pick one goal.

  2. Step 2

    Weekly lessons

    One live lesson a week and a practice plan for the days in between.

  3. Step 3

    Masterclass and ensemble

    Once a month, play with other students in a small online group.

  4. Step 4

    Stage night

    The term ends with a concert, online or at one of our partner venues.

Questions before the first lesson

Something else on your mind? Write to us and a mentor answers, usually the same day.

Ask a mentor

What do I need for an online lesson?

Your instrument, a laptop or tablet with a camera, and headphones. We send a five-minute setup guide that makes the sound clear enough for both of you to play together.

How young can a student start?

Piano from six, guitar and drums from eight, voice from ten. Younger children are welcome in a trial lesson with a parent beside them.

Can I switch mentors?

Yes, at any time and without a reason. Your notes and recordings move to the new mentor so nothing starts again from zero.

How does the free lesson work?

You pick a time, we match you with a mentor, and you spend thirty minutes playing and talking about goals. No card is needed to book it.

Can I pause my plan?

Pause for up to two months a year from your account. Lessons you have already paid for wait for you.
