We programme films the way you tell a secret: quickly, in the dark, to a room that leans in. Twenty minutes is enough to break a heart, start an argument or change a mind.

Cut Short began as one night in a borrowed church hall with a bedsheet for a screen. Fourteen years on it fills three cinemas, but the rule hasn't changed: nothing over twenty minutes, and every film gets a full house.

Ruaridh BellFestival director

We watch every submission to its last frame. What reaches the screen is the work that made us forget to check the time.

Made something under twenty minutes? Send it to us.

Fiction, documentary, animation and everything in between, finished after 1 January 2026. Entry is free for students and first-time filmmakers.

Submit a film

  1. Step 1 · by 1 August

    Send a private link

    A password-protected link, one still and a two-line synopsis. English subtitles for any other language.

  2. Step 2 · August

    Watched to the last frame

    Two programmers watch every film from the first second to the end credits. Nobody skips to the middle.

  3. Step 3 · by 15 September

    Hear back from us

    Everyone gets an answer. Selected filmmakers get a pass, two nights in Glasgow and a seat at the awards.

Questions

[Before you come]

Are the screenings accessible?

All three venues have step-free access and hearing loops. Every programme has one captioned screening, and the Thursday matinees are relaxed screenings with the lights half up.

Is there an age limit?

Most programmes are for 15 and over. After Dark is 18 and over, and Sunday morning Animation is open to anyone aged eight or older.

Are films subtitled?

Every film not in English has English subtitles. Captioned screenings are marked in the printed programme and on each ticket.

Can I get a refund?

Passes can be refunded until 1 November. Day tickets can be moved to another day up to an hour before the first screening.

Will the filmmakers be there?

Most screenings end with a short conversation with the people who made the films, and the festival bar at Trongate is where everyone ends up afterwards.
