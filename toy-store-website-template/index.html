Questions grown-ups ask

Kids mostly ask where the toilet is. It's upstairs, past the puzzles.

Can kids play with the toys in the shop?

Yes. Everything with a “try me” tag is open, and the play table upstairs always has something set up.

Do you post orders?

We post anywhere in the UK. Call or email with what you're after and we'll send a payment link and a photo of the toy.

Does the swap box take toys you didn't sell?

It does, as long as they are clean, working and safe. We don't take soft toys for swapping, but the toy hospital will see them.

Is the tinker table really free?

Really free. Children under eight need a grown-up nearby, and there are twelve seats, so come early for the popular ones.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

From £10, on a card the kids can actually read. They never expire, like the swap box credit.
