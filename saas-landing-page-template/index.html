Questions, answered plainly.

Still unsure? Write to hello@murmur.example and a person replies within a day.

What does murmur need access to?

Read-only access to your shop, your card reader and the inbox labels you choose. It cannot post, sell, refund or change a price.

Why only one sentence?

Because a small shop has time for one change a week. murmur ranks everything it noticed and sends the move most likely to matter; the rest is one tap away if you are curious.

What if I disagree with the note?

Tap Not for us. murmur asks nothing more, remembers the answer and weighs that kind of advice less next time.

Which shops and tills does it work with?

The common online shop platforms and card readers connect in a few minutes. Market sales can be added from a photo of your notebook or a spreadsheet.

Is my data sold or shared?

Never. Your numbers are used only to write your notes, stored in the EU, and deleted the day you close your account.

Can I pause it for the holidays?

Yes. Pause for up to eight weeks and you are not billed; murmur keeps listening quietly and sends one catch-up note when you return.
