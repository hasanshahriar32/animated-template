Before you ride

Still wondering? Call the showroom on 020 0000 0000.

Do I need a licence?

A Hush is a moped: it tops out at 45 km/h. With a car licence issued before 2001 you can ride straight away; otherwise a one-day CBT is enough.

How far does one battery go?

About 80 km of town riding on the H1 and H2, and 160 km on the H3 with both bays filled. Cold mornings take a little off.

Where do I charge it?

From any ordinary three-pin socket. The battery lifts out by its handle, so it can charge at your desk or by the sofa.

What does servicing cost?

The first service is free. After that a visit to your door is £59, or included in the monthly plan.

Can I try one first?

Yes, free, for an hour: from the Hackney showroom, or delivered to your street anywhere inside zone 2.

What if it's stolen?

Every Hush has a tracker and an immobiliser that locks the hub motor. Monthly plans include theft cover.
