A group of twelve, one evening a week, ninety minutes each. You leave every session with a drill for the next seven days.

  5. Week 5

    Joy

    Noticing it while it happens, not three days later.

  6. Week 6

    All of them

    Mixed feelings, real conversations and your own weekly routine.

Fair questions

Anything else? Ask it on Thursday Live; the best questions become episodes.

Is this therapy?

No. It is training for everyday feelings, led by certified coaches. If you are struggling, we will help you find a therapist, and you are welcome in class alongside it.

Do I have to talk in class?

Never. You can listen, write in the chat or keep your journal page to yourself. Most people speak up by week three, on their own terms.

What if I miss a week?

Each session is recorded for your group, and the drill for the week arrives by email, so you can catch up before the next evening.

Can my company pay?

Yes. We invoice in your company's name, and many members use their learning budget for the course.

Is the live show really free?

Yes, every Thursday, with no sign-up. Recordings stay online for a month.
