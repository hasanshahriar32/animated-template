27 Ash Row, Edinburgh. Open Tuesday to Sunday.

We stock what we would write with ourselves, and you can try all of it before it goes in the bag.

Quire began in 2014 as one shelf of Italian notebooks inside a bookshop on Leith Walk. Today it fills a whole shopfront on Ash Row: cotton papers, pencils from three continents, a wall of inks and a counter where every nib can be tested. Nothing goes on a shelf until one of us has filled a page with it.

Five shelves, chosen by hand.

Everything in the shop sits on one of five shelves. We keep each small enough to know by heart, and restock it from makers we have met.

  1. Paper & card

    Cotton rag, laid, kraft and Japanese papers by the sheet, card from 120 to 600 gsm and envelopes to match.

  2. Pens & ink

    Fountain pens to try at the counter, dip pens, fineliners and a wall of forty bottled inks.

  3. Notebooks

    Hand-bound, stitched and lay-flat books in every ruling: plain, lined, squared and dotted.

  4. Post & sealing

    Writing sets, envelopes, sealing wax, brass seals and stamps for the letters people keep.

  5. Paint & drawing

    Pencils in every grade, watercolour pans, sable brushes and sketchbooks that take a wash.

Printed to order, in the back room.

Letterpress cards, invitations and notepaper on a 1960s platen press. Small runs from fifty sheets, in four steps.

Start a print order

  1. Step 1

    Choose a stock, set the type

    Pick a board from the paper library. We set your words in metal or wood type, or make a plate from your own drawing.

  2. Step 2

    Proof and adjust

    A proof reaches you within three working days. We change spacing, colour and position until it is right.

  3. Step 3

    Print by hand

    Every sheet is fed into the press by hand, one colour at a time. Expect a soft, deep bite into the paper.

  4. Step 4

    Wrap and collect

    Wrapped in tissue and kraft, ready to collect on Ash Row, or posted anywhere in the UK.

Can I try a pen before I buy it?

Yes. Every pen we sell has a tester at the counter, with a pad of the papers we stock. Bring your own notebook if you want to see how a nib behaves on it.

Do you post outside the UK?

We post across the UK in two to three working days and to most of Europe in about a week. Bottled ink cannot fly, so it travels by road only.

Can you repair a fountain pen?

We clean, flush and adjust nibs in the shop, usually while you wait. Older pens that need new sacs or parts go to a restorer we trust in York.

Can I pause the Monthly Quire?

Pause, skip or stop it by email before the last Monday of the month. Nothing is charged for a month you skip.

How small can a print run be?

Fifty cards or sheets is our smallest run. Most wedding orders fall between eighty and two hundred.

Are the evenings for complete beginners?

Yes. Every evening starts from the first stroke, and every material is included in the price.
