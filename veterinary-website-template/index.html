Come in. Here’s how a visit goes.

  1. Step 1

    Check in from the car

    Text us when you park. We call you in when your room is ready, so nobody waits nose to nose.

  2. Step 2

    Thirty minutes, on the floor

    Every exam is booked for half an hour. We start on the floor with treats and only use the table if your animal is happy there.

  3. Step 3

    A plan and a price first

    Before any test or treatment you get a written plan with the price. Nothing extra happens without your yes.

  4. Step 4

    A text the next morning

    Your vet checks in the day after and answers questions by text for a week, at no charge.

What we do here.

Everything a family animal needs under one roof, and a referral letter written the same day when it needs more.

  1. Check-ups

    A nose-to-tail exam once a year, twice once they are older. Weight, teeth, heart and joints go into one written record, so you can see the trend.

    30 minutes, any weekday

  2. Vaccines and microchips

    Only the vaccines your animal's life calls for. An indoor cat and a dog who swims in the Willamette do not get the same list.

    Given during a check-up

  3. Teeth

    Cleaning and X-rays under anaesthesia, extractions when a tooth is past saving, and a photo of every tooth we took out.

    Home by the afternoon

  4. Surgery

    Spays, neuters, lumps and stitches, with a nurse who watches nothing but the anaesthetic. We call you when they wake up.

    Tuesdays and Thursdays

  5. Same-day sick visits

    Four slots every day stay empty until morning. Call before ten and a vet sees your animal that day.

    Call before 10 am

  6. The older years

    Pain plans for stiff joints, kidney checks, and when the time comes, a quiet goodbye at home or in our garden room.

    Home visits on request

Before you come in.

Anything else, call 503 000 0000 or write to hello@burrow.example.

Do you see emergencies?

We see sick animals the same day if you call before 10 am. At night and on Sundays we send you to the city's 24-hour emergency hospital and phone ahead with your animal's history.

Can I stay in the room?

For every exam, yes. Most animals are calmer with you there. For X-rays and surgery we take them through, and bring them back as soon as they are awake.

Do you take pet insurance?

We work with every insurer. You pay us on the day and we fill in the claim form before you leave.

What should I bring the first time?

Old records if you have them, the name of the food and any medicines, and something that smells of home: a towel, a toy, a sock.

My cat hates the carrier.

Leave it open in the living room for a week with a towel inside, and cover it on the drive. Our cat-side nurses lift the lid off rather than pulling anyone out.

Can I pay over time?

For bills over $500 you can pay in three monthly parts with no interest. Ask at the desk or mention it when you get your estimate.
