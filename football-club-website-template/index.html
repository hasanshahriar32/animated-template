Kilnfield Colts FC · Armley, Leeds · since 1987

We’re a community football club for 480 boys and girls between five and eighteen. Nobody is cut, nobody spends a season on the bench, and every coach on our touchline is a trained volunteer who stays.

Seven days at Kilnfield

The floodlights are on six nights a week. This is what a week at the ground looks like, from the first cones to the last huddle.

  1. Mon

    Monday

    Girls’ night

    Six girls’ teams train under the lights, one after another until nine.

  2. Tue

    Tuesday

    Keeper school

    One hour, two coaches, every goalkeeper from under 9 up and far too many dives.

  3. Wed

    Wednesday

    Mini Colts

    Cones out at half five. Forty minis, four pitches, nobody standing in a queue.

  4. Thu

    Thursday

    Boot swap

    Outgrown boots come in, the right size goes out. No money changes hands.

  5. Fri

    Friday

    Futsal night

    Indoor five-a-side for under 13s and up, with music and the tuck shop open.

  6. Sat

    Saturday

    Matchday

    Fourteen home games from nine till two, and tea at the hatch all day.

  7. Sun

    Sunday

    Hands in

    Youth and girls’ league games, then the nets come down and the pitch gets a rest.

Four promises

Written by parents in 1987 and still pinned to the clubhouse door. Every coach signs them before their first session.

  1. Everybody plays.

    Up to under 12, every child gets equal minutes. After that, nobody plays less than half a match.

  2. No trials under 13.

    You don’t earn a place in a squad. You turn up, you train, and you’re in the team.

  3. First kit is free.

    Shirt, shorts and socks are on us for the first season. Boots come from the Thursday swap.

  4. Coaches who stay.

    Our coaches stay with their squad for at least two seasons, and most stay a lot longer.

How to join in

From the first email to the first match usually takes about two weeks, and nothing in it costs a penny.

  1. 1

    Book a session

    Pick your child’s age group and a day. No forms, no fee and no commitment.

  2. 2

    Come and play

    Bring shin pads and a water bottle. We lend boots and bibs to anyone who needs them.

  3. 3

    Meet the squad

    The coach introduces your child to the team and walks you through the season.

  4. 4

    First match

    Kit is handed over in the clubhouse, then it’s straight out onto the pitch.

Parents ask

My child has never played. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most Mini Colts have never kicked a ball in a team, and every session starts with games that need no skill whatsoever.

What should they bring to the first session?

Shin pads, a water bottle and trainers or boots. We lend boots, bibs and shin pads to anyone who needs them, no questions asked.

Do you hold trials?

Not below under 13. From under 13 the youth section runs two levels, and players move between them through the season.

How are the coaches checked?

Every coach holds an FA badge, an in-date DBS check and the FA safeguarding certificate. Two adults are at every session.

Can parents stay and watch?

Please do. The touchline is behind the rope, the tea hatch opens at nine and the covered stand is there for wet days.

How can I help the club?

Run a line, wash a kit bag, staff the hatch or take a coaching badge on us. Every role is voluntary and every hour counts.
