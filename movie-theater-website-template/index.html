Still
on film

Most cinemas threw their projectors out years ago. We kept two.

Every week at least one programme runs from a 35mm print: threaded by hand, checked on the rewind bench and changed over by a projectionist who watches for the cue marks. You will see the grain, and now and then a scratch. That is the point.

  • 35mm
  • //
  • 16mm
  • //
  • 4K digital

  1. Two projectors, built in 1979 and serviced every spring.

  2. A feature arrives as five reels. We splice them by hand.

  3. Each print gets the lens and masking its shape needs.

  4. After the last show the print is checked, wound and sent back.

Before
you come

Anything else, ask at the box office or write to hello@halide.example.

Do films start on time?

Yes. We show one short film chosen to go with the feature, then the film. No adverts. If you are late, a member of staff will seat you quietly.

Can I take drinks in?

Anything from our bar, in our glasses. Please keep hot food out of Screen Two: the sofas are old.

Are both screens accessible?

Both have step-free access, wheelchair spaces and a hearing loop. Every Wednesday evening screening is captioned, and relaxed screenings run on Wednesday afternoons.

Can I swap or refund a ticket?

Up to two hours before the film, for anything else or your money back. Members can swap until the doors open.

Why do some films look scratched?

Because they are on film. A 35mm print shows its age a little; we find the best copy we can and handle it with gloves.

Can we hire a screen?

Screen Two seats 48 and can be hired on weekday mornings and Mondays, with your film or one of ours.
