Three fittings, twelve weeks

No coat leaves the workroom on a first try. Each fitting moves it closer to the way you stand, sit and reach, and each is booked around your diary.

  1. Measure

    Step 1Twenty-six measures and notes on how you stand; your pattern is drafted by hand that week.

  2. Baste

    Step 2A coat held together with loose white stitches, opened and chalked again on you.

  3. Forward

    Step 3Pockets, lapels and sleeves in place; the balance and the collar are set.

  4. Finish

    Step 4Hand-sewn buttonholes, a last press, and the suit goes home with you.

Three pairs of hands in every coat

You meet your cutter at every fitting. The coatmaker and the finisher never see you, but they sign the inside pocket.

  • Drafts every pattern by hand and runs all three fittings; twenty-two years at the board.

  • Builds the canvas and shapes the chest of each coat, mostly by hand.

  • Sews the buttonholes, the pick stitching and the linings, one coat at a time.

The Haldane cut

Our house style is close and soft: room where you move, none where you don’t. Four things you will feel before anyone sees them.

  1. Soft shoulder

    A natural line with light padding, so the coat follows your shoulder instead of building a new one.

  2. High armhole

    Cut close under the arm, so you can reach for a glass without the collar leaving your neck.

  3. Long roll

    A lapel that rolls down to the button rather than being pressed flat.

  4. Clean back

    No drag and no ripple across the shoulders: the balance is set at the second fitting.

Questions before a first fitting

Anything else, ask us at the workroom or by email; a cutter answers.

fittings@haldane.example
How long does a suit take?

About twelve weeks from the first measure, with three fittings three to four weeks apart. A second suit from the same pattern takes around eight.

Do I need to come to Edinburgh?

No. Our trunk shows bring the cloth and the fittings to London, Manchester, Copenhagen, New York and Glasgow twice a year.

What makes it bespoke rather than made to measure?

Your pattern is drawn from nothing for you, not adjusted from a standard block, and the coat is basted and refitted on you before it is finished.

Can you copy a suit I already own?

We can take its details as a starting point, but we always cut a new pattern for you rather than tracing the old one.

Do you make for women?

Yes. About a third of the cutting book is suits, coats and trousers for women, cut from the same house style.
