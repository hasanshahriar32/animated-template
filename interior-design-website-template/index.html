Every room is a specimen

We paint for the light you have

A colour is only half paint; the other half is the window. Before we choose anything, we watch your walls at four hours of the day.

  1. MorningCool and clear. Blues look their bluest.
  2. NoonFlat and honest. The hour to judge a white.
  3. EveningLow and warm. Pinks blush and greys turn to sand.
  4. NightLamps only. Now the ceiling sets the mood.

Four sittings

Every commission takes four meetings, never more. Between them, we draw.

  1. Sitting 1

    Walk

    We walk your rooms at two hours of the day with paint cards and a notebook. You talk, we listen.

  2. Sitting 2

    Draw

    A specimen sheet: plan, palette, shapes and lamps on one page, with a sample of every finish.

  3. Sitting 3

    Sample

    Swatches a metre square on your own walls, seen in the morning and again at night.

  4. Sitting 4

    Paint

    We stay with the painters until the last line of tape comes off, then leave a tin of each shade.

Questions people ask

Anything else, ask us on a colour walk or write a note below.

Do you work outside Lyon?

Yes. Colour walks are within two hours of Lyon by train. Drawings and light scores can be made anywhere from your photos, plans and a video call.

Can we keep our furniture?

Almost always. We draw around the pieces you love and tell you honestly which one is fighting the room.

How long does a one-room drawing take?

Three weeks from the first sitting to the specimen sheet, then usually two more until the painters have packed up.

Do you sell the paints?

Only within a commission, mixed to order in Lyon. Each project ends with a tin of every shade we used.

What if we hate the colour?

That is what the third sitting is for. We paint swatches a metre square on your own walls and change them until you stop frowning.
