Questions people ask at the desk

Something else? Call 0161 000 0000 any day from 7:00 to 21:00.

What can't I store?

Anything alive, perishable, flammable or illegal: food, paint thinners, gas bottles, fuel and cash. Everything else is fine, from a piano to a sock drawer.

Do I need my own insurance?

No. Contents cover up to £2,000 is in the price. Raise it to £50,000 from £1 a week, or use your home policy if it covers items in storage.

Can someone else visit my unit?

Yes. Share access in the app with anyone you like and take it back whenever you want. Every visit is logged.

How do I pay?

By card or direct debit, monthly in advance. Business customers get VAT invoices.

What if I need a bigger unit?

Tell us in the app or at the desk. If one is free we move your things across the same week, at no cost.

How do I leave?

Give fourteen days' notice, empty the unit and sweep it out. We charge to the day you hand back access, not to the end of the month.
