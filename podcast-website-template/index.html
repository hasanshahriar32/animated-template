This week: the bridge that sings

Mira Okafor spent four years listening to one footbridge. Dan asked her everything. Here is the part everyone quotes.

Listen to episode 41

Small Bridges · Ep. 41 · 38 min

The bridge that sings

with Mira Okafor, acoustic engineer

00:4212:31

  1. Dan · 00:42

    So the bridge actually sings? Like, a melody?

  2. Mira · 01:10

    Not a melody. One note, a low B flat, whenever the wind comes from the north-east faster than thirty kilometres an hour.

  3. Dan · 03:55

    And people noticed before the engineers did.

  4. Mira · 04:20

    A cellist who walked her dog there every morning. She wrote to the city: your bridge is out of tune.

  5. Dan · 12:08

    Did they fix it?

  6. Mira · 12:31

    They tuned it. Four dampers, a lot of arguing, and now it hums a perfect fifth lower. People come on windy days just to listen.

Questions people ask before they press play

Anything else goes to the inbox below, and a person answers.

Is Gabble free to listen to?

Yes. Every show outside Plus is free, in our app and in any podcast app, with one short sponsor message per episode at most.

What does Plus cost?

Four euros a month or forty a year. The first month is free and you can cancel in two taps.

How do authors get paid?

Seventy percent of Plus income goes to authors, split by minutes listened, paid every month. Free shows share sponsor income the same way.

Can I use the studio before I am on Gabble?

Send a pilot first, recorded on anything. Once a show is accepted, its author books the studio like everyone else.

Which apps can I listen in?

Our own app on phones and the web, and every podcast app that reads a feed. Plus shows get a private feed of their own.
