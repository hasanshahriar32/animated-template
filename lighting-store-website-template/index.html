Before the lamps arrive

Short answers to what people ask at the counter. Anything else, write to us.

Do you deliver, and how fast?

Across the UK in three to five working days, packed in paper and card. Glasgow orders over £150 come in our own van, often the next day.

Can you fit the lamps as well?

Yes. Our electrician fits pendants and wall lights across Glasgow from £65 a visit. Plug-in lamps need nobody.

What does a lighting plan cost?

Nothing with an order over £600. Otherwise it is £90, and the £90 comes off any lamps you buy from the plan within a year.

Which bulb should I buy?

For living rooms and bedrooms, warm white and dimmable. Bring the old bulb in or send a photo of the fitting, and we will match the base and the light.

Can I return a lamp?

Within thirty days, unused and in its box. Made-to-order shades and repaired lamps cannot come back, and we say so before you pay.
