[The studio]

One car in the studio at a time.

The bay at Example Works holds one car. Nothing else is sanded, polished or sprayed in the room while yours is there; the air is filtered, the floor is heated, and the lamps are the colour of a clear afternoon.

  • Daylight lamps on three sides of the car
  • Coatings cure at a steady 20 °C
  • Collection and return within 30 miles

[Five days]

A working week for one car.

A full treatment runs Monday to Friday. At the end of each day you get a photo and the readings, so you know where the car is without having to ask.

  1. Monday

    Inspect and measure

    Lamps, gauge and notes. Every chip, scratch and respray is photographed and agreed with you before we start.

  2. Tuesday

    Wash and decontaminate

    Snow foam, a two-bucket wash, iron fallout and tar dissolved, then clay on every panel until the paint feels like glass.

  3. Wednesday

    Correct

    Compound and polish, panel by panel, re-reading the gauge as we go. The day ends with a look under every lamp.

  4. Thursday

    Coat

    A panel wipe, then two layers of ceramic, levelled by hand and cured under infrared overnight.

  5. Friday

    Finish and hand over

    Glass, wheels and cabin, a last look under the lamps, and the keys handed back with the readings and an aftercare kit.

[Questions]

What owners ask before booking.

How long will you have my car?

Five working days for a full treatment, one to three for a single one. We agree the dates at the inspection and keep them.

Will every scratch come out?

We remove what the clear coat allows. A scratch through to the colour can be made much less visible, not erased — we show you which is which under the lamps before we start.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Ours carries a five-year warranty with a free yearly check. How long it really lasts depends on washing, so we show you how at handover.

Can I still use a car wash?

Please avoid brushes. A touchless jet wash is fine in a pinch; a two-bucket hand wash, or our maintenance wash, is better.

Do you collect the car?

Yes, on a covered trailer, within thirty miles of Manchester. It is free with any five-day treatment.

What does an inspection cost?

Nothing. It takes about forty minutes, and you leave with your paint readings whether you book or not.

[Aftercare]

A coating is not armour. Wash it gently, dry it softly, and the gloss you collected is the gloss you keep.

  • Two buckets, a clean mitt, never a brush.
  • Rinse before you touch; dry with a plush towel.
  • Bring it back once a year — the check is free.
