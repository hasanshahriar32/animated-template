What we help with

The five things parents ask us about most. Each card shows something a child might say today — and the word we're working towards.

  • First words

    Today: ba Soon: ball, please

    For toddlers who understand plenty but say little. We build sounds, signs and single words, then put two together.

    From 18 months to 4 years

  • Speech sounds

    Today: tat Soon: cat

    Sounds swapped, missed or muddled — lisps, "w" for "r", "t" for "k". We train the ear first, then the mouth.

    From 3 to 11 years

  • Stammering

    Today: I w-w-want Soon: my go!

    Calm, practical help for children who get stuck, repeat or stretch sounds — and for the grown-ups who listen.

We talk, play, plan and practise.

From the first phone call to the weekly routine, here's what happens and how much of your time each step takes.

  1. Step 1 · 15 minutes

    A free call

    Tell us what you've noticed. We'll say honestly whether to wait and watch or book an assessment, and nobody will chase you either way.

  2. Step 2 · 75 minutes

    A play-based assessment

    On the floor with toys, books and bubbles while we listen and watch. You stay in the room the whole time.

  3. Step 3 · within a week

    A plan you can read

    Two or three goals in plain English, how we'll work on them, and what "better" will look like at home.

  4. Step 4 · every week

    Sessions, then ten minutes a day

    Forty-five minutes with the same therapist, plus a short game for each day. We review the plan every six sessions.

Play is the method, not the reward.

Children talk most when they're busy having fun. Every game in our playrooms is chosen for the sounds and words it makes easy.

  • Sock puppets. Taking turns — my go, your go — without anyone being told to.

  • A toy phone. First conversations, with no pressure to look at anyone.

  • Bubbles. Round lips for "oo" and "w", and a very good reason to say "more".

  • Den reading. Naming, pointing and waiting for the next page.

Ten minutes a day, and someone to ask.

Between sessions you get a short game for each day and a direct line to your own therapist. Most questions are answered the same day.

  • A game for each day, sent after every session
  • Messages answered by your therapist, not a call centre
  • A short video whenever a new sound needs showing

Fees, plainly

No packages to sign up to, and no charge for cancelling with 48 hours' notice. You pay after each session.

Do we need a referral?

No. Parents can book with us directly. If your GP or health visitor has written to you, bring the letter along.

What if my child won't join in?

That's common, and it tells us something too. The first session is play on their terms, and you stay in the room.

How many sessions will we need?

It varies. We review the plan every six sessions and tell you honestly when it's time to stop.

Can we claim the cost back?

Some health insurers cover therapy from an HCPC-registered therapist. You'll get an itemised receipt for every session.
