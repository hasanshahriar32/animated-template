Where Our Metal Comes From

Every gram we work has been gold before. We would rather refine a ring you'll never wear again than buy metal fresh from the ground — and we can show you the paper trail for every stone we set.

Read Our Questions

  • Your Own Gold

    Tested and refined in our studio, alloyed back to 9, 14 or 18 carat. You get your own metal back, not an equivalent.

  • Recycled Bullion

    When yours runs short, we top it up with recycled grain from a British refiner, hallmarked at the Edinburgh Assay Office.

  • Traceable Stones

    Diamonds and sapphires with a record back to the mine, or the stones you bring, re-cut only if they need it.

Five Sittings From Drawer To Hand

Most commissions take six to eight weeks. You're welcome at all five sittings; most people come to three and leave the rest to us.

  1. 1

    Sit & Sketch

    An hour at the bench with the goldsmith who will make your piece. We draw it around your hand, not from a catalogue.

  2. 2

    Melt & Refine

    Your gold is tested, weighed and melted while you watch, then alloyed to the carat and colour you choose.

  3. 3

    Saw & Forge

    The metal is rolled, sawn and forged into shape. Come back and hold it before the stone goes in.

  4. 4

    Set The Stone

    Each claw is raised and closed under the loupe. A solitaire takes our setter most of an afternoon.

  5. 5

    Polish & Hand Over

    Polished, hallmarked and handed to you across the same bench it was made on, with a note of its weight and story.

Asked At The Counter

Something else? Call the studio on 0131 000 0000 and Morag will answer.

How long does a commission take?

Six to eight weeks from the first sketch for most rings; wedding bands about four. If there's a date, tell us at the first sitting and we'll plan backwards from it.

What if my gold isn't enough for the piece?

We weigh it at the valuation and top it up with recycled gold. You pay only for the difference, priced per gram on the day.

Will my piece be hallmarked?

Yes. Everything over the legal weight is sent to the Edinburgh Assay Office and comes back with its hallmark before you collect it.

Do you buy gold too?

We do, at the day's rate, and we'll always give you the figure in writing. Most people decide to remake instead once they've seen the sketches.

Can I see the work in progress?

Every commission includes a visit to approve the wax model and another before the stone is set. Many clients drop in more often; the kettle's always on.
