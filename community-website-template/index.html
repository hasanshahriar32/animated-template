The softlife principle

We think the good life is not a race. Rest is a strategy. Softness is a strength. A slow morning is a perfectly serious plan, and the best ideas turn up somewhere between the second coffee and the long way home. So we built a house for doing less, beautifully, together.

Founded in 2019 by Margot Ellery and eleven very tired friends. Eight hundred members later, the chairs are still the point.

Dear you,

I started softlife in 2019 after a year of saying yes to everything and enjoying almost none of it. I wanted a place where ambitious women could rest without apologising — somewhere with good chairs, better soup and a ban on the word hustle.

Six years on there are eight hundred of us. We run companies, raise children, write books and occasionally do absolutely nothing. All of it counts.

Come for a slow morning. Stay for supper.

Margot

Margot Ellery · Founder

Good questions

Anything else, write to us — a real person answers within a day.

hello@softlife.example
Do I need to be a member to visit?

No. A day pass gets you through the door for any one session plus the reading room. Most members started that way.

Who is the house for?

The house is built for women and non-binary people. Guests of any gender are welcome at supper club and on open evenings.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes, for up to three months a year and without a fee. Life happens; so do long holidays.

Is there a joining fee?

No joining fee and no minimum term. Cancel any time with thirty days' notice.

Can I bring my laptop?

In Office Hours and the garden, yes. The reading room and the supper table are screen-free.

Do you host private events?

Founding circle members can host suppers for up to twenty guests. Ask us about birthdays and book launches.
