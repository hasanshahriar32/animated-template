Questions people ask before the first call

Or ask us yourself: 0113 000 0000

Do you really not charge a fee?

For most residential mortgages, no. The lender pays us a fee when your mortgage completes, usually 0.35% to 0.45% of the loan, and we tell you the exact amount in writing before you apply. Complex cases, such as limited-company buy-to-let, carry a £495 fee that we agree with you first.

Will talking to you affect my credit score?

No. We use a soft search to see your file the way a lender will, and it leaves no footprint that other lenders can see. A hard search happens only when you decide to apply.

How much deposit do I need?

Some lenders accept 5%, but 10% or 15% usually unlocks noticeably better rates. We show you the difference in pounds a month for your own numbers, so you can decide whether waiting to save more is worth it.

I'm self-employed. Can I still get a mortgage?

Yes. Most lenders want two years of accounts or tax calculations, and some accept one. Daniel works with contractors and company directors every week and knows which lenders read accounts sensibly.

How long from first call to mortgage offer?

An agreement in principle usually takes a day. Once your offer on a home is accepted, a full mortgage offer typically takes two to four weeks, depending on the lender and the valuation.

What happens when my fixed rate ends?

We call you six months before, compare the market again and reserve a new rate. If rates fall before your switch date, we re-book the lower one, so you never spend a month on the standard variable rate.
