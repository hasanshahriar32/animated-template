Season fifty, same maple floor

We laid sixteen hundred maple boards in the spring of 1976 and opened on the first warm Friday in May.

The mirror ball went up three years later. Since then the floor has been sanded forty-nine times, the snack bar has changed its fryer twice, and Friday nights have never once been called off for lack of skaters. This summer the doors open for the fiftieth time.

Sixteen hundred boards of hard maple

The floor is the rink. Ours is northern hard maple, tongue and groove, laid over a sprung timber frame so your knees forgive the falls.

  • Sprung, not slab

    Timber joists under every board give a softer landing than concrete.

  • Sealed every August

    We close from 4 to 18 August, sand it back and lay seven coats.

  • Skates only past the rail

    No street shoes on the maple, ever. Socks are fine for birthday photos.

Fifty seasons on the same floor

A roof, two fryers, one rescue and a lot of records. The boards never changed.

  1. 1976

    Doors open

    The first Friday in May: four hundred skaters, an organ player and a floor that still smelled of sealant.

  2. 1979

    The mirror ball goes up

    Bought second-hand from a hotel ballroom. It still turns on its original motor.

  3. 1984

    Neon over the snack bar

    Pink and teal tubes, a new fryer and the rule about no skates on the carpet.

  4. 1993

    The inline summer

    Everyone bought blades. By autumn half of them were back on quads.

  5. 2008

    A new roof, the same floor

    Three weeks closed, every board under tarpaulin, not one replaced.

  6. 2014

    The rink is saved

    The lease ran out. Six hundred regulars bought the building back, one share each.

  7. 2020

    A quiet season

    Closed from March to September. We sanded the floor twice out of boredom.

  8. 2026

    Season fifty

    Opening night on 1 May with the 1976 setlist and admission at 1976 prices.

Records from the booth

Friday nights are vinyl only. Dee Marlowe has run the booth since 1988 and keeps four crates under the counter, one for each era of the rink.

  1. Crate one: 1976 to 1983

    Disco, Philly soul and roller boogie. The couples’ skate always comes from this crate.

  2. Crate two: the eighties

    Electro, freestyle and synth-pop. It plays when the lights go pink.

  3. Crate three: the nineties

    House and hip-hop for the inline summer and every summer after.

  4. Crate four: requests

    Anything you ask for, as long as it is on vinyl. Leave a card at the booth or send it from here.

Before your first lap

Still wondering? Call the counter during any session and someone will pick up between songs.

Do I need my own skates?

No. Rental quads are included in every ticket. Your own quads or inline skates are welcome if the wheels are clean and the toe stops are not metal.

Is there a minimum age?

Children of any age can skate with an adult on the floor. Lessons start at six, and Tuesday nights are eighteen and over.

Do I have to wear a helmet?

Helmets are required for under-twelves and lent free at the counter. Wrist guards are a good idea for anyone on their first few sessions.

Can I just watch?

Yes, the benches along the rail are free for anyone with a skater in the family. The snack bar is open to everyone.

Can I pay at the door?

Always. Cash or card, no booking needed for public sessions. Courses and parties are booked ahead.
