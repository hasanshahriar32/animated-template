We're Ailsa and Dev. We fixed our first cabinet on a kitchen table in Partick.

Quarter Moon opened in 2019 under two railway arches with eleven machines bought from a seaside arcade that was closing for good.

Now the floor holds forty-two, the long bar pours till two and the back room rebuilds whatever the league breaks. Same rule as night one: if it's lit, it plays.
