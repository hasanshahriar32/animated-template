Most break-ins at small offices start with one reused password or one convincing email. We put five layers in the way and set each one up at the person’s own desk.

Questions offices ask before they call.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the audit; there are no wrong questions about printers.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. You pay monthly and can leave with thirty days’ notice. When you go, we hand over every password and a written map of your setup.

What if something breaks at night?

Broadband, server and security alerts reach whoever is on call. A laptop that will not start on a Saturday can wait for Monday, and we will be with you by nine.

We already have someone who does our IT.

Keep them if they enjoy it. Most people we meet would rather stop; we can take the dull part and leave them the projects they like.

Do you come to the office?

Yes, anywhere within an hour of Nottingham, as often as it is needed. Most fixes still happen remotely within the same morning.

We use Macs. Is that a problem?

Not at all. About a third of our offices are mostly Mac, and most of the rest have a mix of both.
