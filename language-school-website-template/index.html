From hello to holding your own

Five levels, each about ten weeks of evenings. Your free class tells us where you start; you move up when you can say the things on the next bar.

  1. A1

    First words

    Greet people, order, ask the way and spell your name.

  2. A2

    Getting by

    Talk about your week, book a room and sort out a mix-up.

  3. B1

    Holding a conversation

    Tell a story, give an opinion and follow a podcast.

  4. B2

    Arguing about films

    Disagree politely, explain why and land a small joke.

  5. C1

    Thinking in it

    Work, negotiate and dream in it, and start missing English words.

A week at Saylo

Classes are only half of it. The rest of the week is for using what you learned somewhere that is not a classroom.

  • Mon to Thu

    Evening classes

    At 18:30 and 20:00, ninety minutes each, two evenings a week for ten weeks.

  • Friday

    Conversation clubs

    One table per language at Café Lindo on Call Lane, from six. Teachers drop in; nobody corrects you unless you ask.

  • Saturday

    Market walk

    Ten till twelve at Kirkgate Market, shopping and haggling in the language you are learning.

  • Online

    Any evening, from home

    Every class streams, so a late shift or a sick child does not cost you the lesson.

Questions people ask before the first class

Anything else, ask us at the front desk or on the phone.

I have never learned a language. Where do I start?

At a free class. You sit in on a beginners’ group, the teacher hears you say a few things, and we suggest a level and an evening that suits you.

What if I miss a class?

Every class streams, so you can join from home, or come to another group at the same level that week.

How big are the groups?

Never more than six people. Small enough that everyone talks in every class, big enough to have someone to argue with.

Who are the teachers?

People who grew up speaking the language and trained with us to teach it. Most have lived in Leeds for years and teach the language as it is spoken now.

Do you teach teams at work?

Yes. We run courses for teams in your office or ours, built around the conversations your people actually have.
