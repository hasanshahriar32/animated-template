09:00, at the farm

12:00, at the packhouse

Out on the lanes

Six electric vans leave together and split by postcode. The drivers know which gates stick.

15:00, on the road

On your step

Before supper, with a text when it lands. Leave last week's crate out for us.

By 19:00, at your door

The box comes back.

Leave last week's crate on the step and the driver takes it home. We wash it, stack it and send it out again; a crate does about forty trips before it is mended or chipped for mulch.

How returns work

The asterisk is the promise, not the small print.

  1. Nothing flown in.

    If it can't come by road from within forty miles, it isn't in the box. That's why there are no bananas.

  2. Nothing heated under glass.

    Tunnels keep the rain off; they never burn gas. Tomatoes arrive in July, not in March.

  3. No plastic, not even the tape.

    Paper bags, a waxed cotton wrap for the leaves, and a crate that comes back.

  4. Growers set the price.

    Each farm names its price before the season starts, and the box price is built up from there.

  5. Picked the day it travels.

    Leaves and roots leave the field the morning they reach you. Only squash and onions are cured, on the farm.

  6. Skip or stop any week.

    No contract and no minimum. Pause for a holiday from your account up to Sunday midnight.

Questions, answered plainly

Anything else, write to hello@furrow.example

When does my box arrive?

Each area has one delivery day a week, Tuesday to Friday. Check your postcode below; vans usually reach you between three and seven in the evening.

What if I'm out?

Tell us a safe place when you sign up: a porch, a neighbour, behind the bins. Boxes are packed to sit outside for an evening, even in summer.

Can I choose what goes in?

The box follows what is ready, but you can swap up to three things a week and keep a list of what you never want sent.

How do returns work?

Leave last week's crate on the step; the driver folds it and takes it back. If one goes missing nothing happens, we would just like it back.

Is everything certified organic?

Yes. Every farm we buy from is certified, and each grower's licence number is printed on the recipe card with their name.

How do I pause or cancel?

From your account, any time up to Sunday midnight for the next week. No calls and no forms.

Do our vans come down your lane?
