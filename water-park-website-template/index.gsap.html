The big four

Twenty-two slides in the park. These four are the ones with a queue, and the queue is worth it.

  1. The Kraken

    Thrill Tower

    Trapdoor drop · 18 metres · from 1.3 m tall

    Stand on the trapdoor, hear the countdown, lose the floor. Two seconds of nothing, then the longest scream in Leeds.

  2. Corkscrew

    Thrill Tower

    Twin spiral tubes · 140 metres · from 1.2 m tall

    Two clear tubes wound round each other. Race a friend, watch them through the wall, lose by a splash.

  3. The Long Way Round

    The Lagoon

    Lazy river · 400 metres · all ages

    Grab a ring and let the current do the rest: under the bridge, past the palms and round again.

  4. Big Swell

    The Lagoon

    Wave pool · waves up to 1.8 m · board sessions

    Every ten minutes the horn goes and the deep end stands up. Board sessions on weekday evenings.

buy a band plan the trip

One band. Every slide, all season.

The season wristband is a membership you wear. Tap it at the gate, on a locker and at the café till, from the first weekend of March to the last of October.

  • Every slide, every day

    The Kraken at ten, the lazy river at four, the Kraken again at five to close.

  • First in

    Band holders get the tower to themselves for the half hour before the gates open.

  • Locker on us

    Tap any free locker and it is yours until you tap it again.

  • Plus-one Tuesdays

    Bring a friend every Tuesday in term time. They swim free; you look generous.

  • Chips on the wrist

    Pay at the café with a tap and settle up at the gate on the way out.

  • Pause any time

    Broken arm, new baby, exams: freeze the band for up to eight weeks a season.

Before you jump

The questions the front desk hears most, answered once and properly.

How tall do I need to be for the big slides?

The Kraken and Corkscrew need 1.3 metres, most other tower slides 1.2. There is a measuring post at the bottom of every staircase, and staff at the top check again.

Can we bring our own food?

Yes. Picnics are welcome on the terrace and in the family area; please leave glass at home. The café does hot food and coffee from 10:00.

Do children need an adult in the water?

Under-eights need one adult in the water for every two children. Under-twelves can swim on their own as long as an adult is in the building.

Can I pause my season band?

Yes. Injury, illness, exams or a new baby: email the desk and we freeze it for up to eight weeks a season.

How warm is the water?

The lagoon and the slide pools sit at 30 °C, the hot pool at 34 °C. The roof opens on warm days and closes when it rains.

Is the park step-free?

From the car park to the poolside, yes, with a pool hoist, a changing places room and free entry for carers.
