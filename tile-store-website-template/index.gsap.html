Before you order

Ask a fitter

028 9000 0000

How many tiles do I need?

Measure each wall or floor, multiply, and add a tenth for cuts and breakages, or a seventh for diagonal and herringbone. Bring the numbers in and we check them against a setting-out plan.

Can I return what I don't use?

Unopened boxes, within 60 days, with the receipt. Tiles cut to order and clearance lines cannot come back.

Do you deliver to Great Britain?

Yes, in three working days on a pallet. Northern Ireland is next working day, and Belfast addresses can choose a two-hour slot.

Which tiles can go on a shower floor?

Anything rated R10 or higher for slip: most porcelain, terracotta and our matt cement tiles. Glossy wall tiles stay on the walls.

Do I have to use your fitters?

No. Plenty of builders buy from us, and so do people who tile their own. The setting-out plan comes with the order either way.

Which grout colour should I choose?

Matching grout hides the grid; contrasting grout draws it. Choose with the sample on your wall, not under shop lights.
