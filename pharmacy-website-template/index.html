Independent since 1987, and still nobody's branch

Margaret Hale opened this counter in 1987 with one dispensing bench and one rule: whoever is unwell gets the next free pharmacist, not the next free till. When she retired, three chains offered to buy the shop. Sami and I bought it instead, from the person who trained us both.

Nothing here is decided by a head office. We stock what our patients ask for, keep the clinic room open on Saturdays, and Biscuit, Margaret's dog, still sleeps on the counter in the afternoons. If something here could be better, tell me at the counter.

Asked at the counter

The questions we hear most, each filed under the part of the pharmacy that answers it.

Repeats
Can you order my repeat from the surgery for me?

Yes. Tell us once which surgery you use and what you take. We request each repeat seven days before you run out and text you when it is packed.

Repeats
What happens to my weekly pack if a dose changes?

We repack it the same day your GP sends the change, and swap the old pack at your door or at the counter. You never have to pick tablets out yourself.

Clinic
Do I need an appointment for the walk-in room?

No. Come in, tell the counter what is wrong, and the next free pharmacist sees you, usually within twenty minutes. Jabs are the only thing we book ahead.

Clinic
Is the flu jab free for me?

It is free at 65 and over, in pregnancy, for carers and with most long-term conditions. Ring us with your situation and we will tell you before you come in.

Shop
Can you order something you don't stock?

Usually by the next afternoon. Ask at the counter or by email, and we will tell you the price before we order.

People
Can I speak to a pharmacist in private?

Always. Ask for the clinic room, or just say you would rather talk somewhere else. Nobody at the counter will ask you why.
