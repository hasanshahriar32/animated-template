Four rides from the stands

All four are loaded on the bike's screen. Distance, climb and time are at an easy pace on the middle assist level.

Five Weirs

From stand B · 11 km · 40 m of climb · about an hour

Down the River Don past the old steelworks and five weirs to Meadowhall, back on the other bank. Flat enough for a first ride.

Canal basin

From stand A · 9 km · 20 m of climb · 50 minutes

From the station to Victoria Quays, then the towpath east to the Tinsley locks. Narrowboats, herons and one café barge.

The Edge

From stand D · 32 km · 480 m of climb · three hours

Up the Porter valley and out of the city onto the moor below Stanage Edge. The one climb people come back to brag about.

Ladybower

From stand E · 46 km · 620 m of climb · four hours

Out along the Loxley valley, round the reservoirs and back. Take a Climber: two batteries cover it with room to spare.

Three steps to a ride

Book online or walk up to any stand: we keep a few bikes back every morning for walk-ups.

  1. 1

    Book a slot

    Choose a bike, a stand and a start time. You pay when you collect, and you can cancel free up to an hour before.

  2. 2

    Collect and set up

    Show your booking and a photo ID. We fit the helmet, set the saddle and walk you through the three assist levels in five minutes.

  3. 3

    Ride, return anywhere

    Leave the bike at any of the five stands before it closes. Battery getting low on the way? Swap it at whichever stand you pass.

Questions

Anything else: ring the Station Arches stand, someone is in the workshop all day.

Do I need to be fit to ride one?

No. The motor helps as soon as you pedal, so you choose how much effort to put in. On the highest assist level most riders climb our steepest streets seated.

How old do riders have to be?

Sixteen or over to ride on your own. Children from one to seven can ride on the Hauler's seats with a parent pedalling.

What if the battery runs low?

The screen shows the range left. Ride to any stand and we swap the battery in a minute, free. A flat battery still leaves you a normal bike with light gears.

Can I take a bike on the train?

The Pocket folds and travels as luggage on every train. The other bikes go on Northern services outside the rush hours, space permitting.

Do you take a deposit?

We hold £200 on a card while the bike is out and release it when you return it. Nothing is taken unless there is damage beyond the £50 excess.
