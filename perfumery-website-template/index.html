Origins Before the Bottle

Grown farfrom the shop,visited in person /

We buy from six growers and distillers we have stood beside. When a harvest is poor, the scent that needs it simply waits for the next one.

No Window, No Sign

The shop is one room up a stair in Leith.

There are 41 scents on the shelf at the moment, from eleven small houses and our own bench. None of them is on display. They live in a cabinet of numbered drawers and come out one strip at a time, so the room smells of nothing until you ask.

The Accord

Materialand scentare one thing /

A perfume is not a picture of a place. It is a little of the place itself: resin bled from bark, petals pressed before noon, a root left to dry for three summers.

Once it leaves the bench, a scent carries its origin without describing it. That is why we never print the story on the label, and why you meet it on a strip first.

Questions

Asked mostoften atthe table /

What happens in a blind session?

Fifty minutes at the table with a host and a dozen strips. You keep the ones you return to; we reveal names, makers and prices at the end. A session costs £25, taken off any bottle you buy.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. Many people leave with a card of numbers and nothing else. The strips you choose are yours to take home.

Can I smell by post?

Yes. The Blind Set sends six sealed vials with numbered cards, so you can do the same ritual at your own table.

Where do you ship?

Within the UK and the EU, in recyclable card. Extraits travel as liquids, so we pack them by hand on Mondays.

Can I return a bottle?

Unopened bottles within 30 days. Opened ones we cannot sell again, but we will swap them for a set of samples.
