Keep it loud

Vivid colour fades. How fast is up to you: six weeks, five rules, one gloss.

  1. Day 0

    Leave it 72 hours before the first wash.

  2. Week 1

    Wash cool, and half as often as you think.

  3. Week 2

    Sulphate-free shampoo only — we write down which.

  4. Week 4

    Heat protection every time, no exceptions.

  5. Week 6

    Book the twenty-minute gloss. It puts the colour back.
