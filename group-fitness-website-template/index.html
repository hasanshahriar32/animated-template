The fifty-minute arc

Whatever the class and whatever the music, every hour at Highkick has the same shape, so you always know how much is left in the tank.

  1. 0–10 min

    Warm-up

    Marching, step-touches, big arm circles. Your heart rate climbs slowly.

  2. 10–25 min

    Build

    The combinations stack up: four moves, then eight, then the whole routine.

  3. 25–40 min

    Peak

    The chorus: top tempo, biggest moves, the song everyone shouts.

  4. 40–50 min

    Cool-down

    Slow tracks, long stretches and one last big breath together.
