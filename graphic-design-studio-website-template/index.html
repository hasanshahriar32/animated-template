two inks, no excuses

Every job starts with one rule: two inks, one paper.

Limits force decisions. Which word is loud, which line stays quiet, where the second ink lands. A two-colour identity looks deliberate on a phone and on a bus shelter, and it costs less to print.

  1. one paper per jobChosen before the first sketch, so every layout is drawn for its real colour and grain.
  2. two inks, picked togetherThe second ink is decided with the first, never added at the end to brighten things up.
  3. misregistration staysWhen the drums drift a millimetre, we keep it. It is the proof that a person printed this.

how a job goes to print

  1. step 1

    brief over coffee

    We read everything you have, then ask the awkward questions until the job fits on one page. You leave with a sketch on a napkin, not a mood board.

  2. step 2

    sketch in one ink

    Layouts in a single colour first, so the structure carries the idea before colour can hide a weak one.

  3. step 3

    add the second ink

    Colour goes where it earns attention: one word, one shape, one edge. Then we print a proof and argue about it.

  4. step 4

    trim, test, hand over

    Files, guidelines and a box of proofs on your desk, plus a print run from our room if you want one.

questions we get a lot

how long does an identity take?

Eight to twelve weeks from the first coffee to the guidelines. If you want it printed by us, add a week for proofs and the run.

do you only design for print?

No. Most identities live on screens first. We design for print because it keeps the work honest, then build the digital kit from the same rules.

can we print a job you did not design?

Yes. The print room is open to anyone with a file. Book a slot, bring a PDF with one layer per ink, and we will set up the drums with you.

what does a project cost?

Posters start around a week of work, identities around two months. After the first conversation you get a fixed quote on one page, no hourly surprises.

do you work with clients abroad?

Often. We meet on video, post the proofs and ship the final run. Only the coffee for the brief is harder to share.
