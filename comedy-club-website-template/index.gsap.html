House rules (the real ones)

We printed the polite version once. Nobody read it. Here is what actually happens in the arch.

  1. No heckling

    Heckle at your own risk

    The comic has the microphone, the lights and ten years of comebacks. You have half a pint.

  2. Phones on silent

    Phones in the pouch

    We lock them in a pouch at the door. The jokes stay in the room, and so do you.

  3. Sit anywhere

    The front row gets talked to

    Seats are unallocated, so choose like an adult: the front row is part of the show.

  4. Arrive on time

    Latecomers become material

    Walk in after the MC starts and the MC will want to know exactly where you’ve been.

  5. Laugh if it’s funny

    Laugh at the new ones too

    New material is fragile. A kind laugh on a Wednesday builds the hour you’ll pay to see in May.

Asked before

Answered with a straight face, for once.

Is there an age limit?

Every show is 18+ and we ask for ID at the door. Some of the language would make your nan blush. Some of it would make her laugh.

Are the seats allocated?

No. Doors open half an hour before the show and it’s first in, first seated. The front row is a choice, not a punishment. Mostly.

Will I get picked on?

Only if you sit in the front row, arrive late or wear a hat indoors. Sit in the middle and you’re safe, unless you laugh like a goose.

Is the arch accessible?

Yes: a step-free entrance from the wharf, four wheelchair spaces beside the stage, an accessible toilet and a hearing loop. Tell us when you book.

Can I swap or refund tickets?

We swap tickets to another night up to 24 hours before the show. If we cancel, you get your money back the same day.

Can I eat there?

Fries, toasties and pickles from the bar until 21:00 and again after the show. Nothing that crunches during a set, please.
