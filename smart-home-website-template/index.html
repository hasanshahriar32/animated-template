Questions

What people ask on the survey, answered before it.

Do you need to chase walls or lift floors?

In most homes, no. We use existing back boxes and ceiling voids, and wireless controls where a cable cannot reach. New builds and renovations get a fully wired system while the walls are open.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Everything keeps working. Scenes, schedules and plates run on a controller in the house; the internet only adds the app away from home and our remote checks.

Can we keep our switches and radiators?

Usually. Most radiators take a smart valve in minutes, and we can fit dimmers behind your current plates or match new plates to them.

How long does an install take?

Essentials takes about three days on site. A whole house is six to ten working days, phased so you can live there throughout.

Are we tied to you afterwards?

No. We choose open, well-supported systems and explain why in your design. At handover you get every password, drawing and device list.

What does aftercare cost after ten years?

Year eleven onwards is an optional plan at £14 a month, covering remote checks, the annual visit and call-outs.
