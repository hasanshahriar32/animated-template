Six ways to spend an hour

Every group on Spare Hour works on one of six things the city keeps asking for. Pick the one that sounds like you, or try them all in a month.

  • Food

    Larders, community fridges and Saturday lunches.

  • Gardens

    Raised beds, orchards and green corners of the estates.

  • Reading

    After-school reading and English over coffee.

  • Company

    Tea tables, phone calls and walks with older neighbours.

  • Repairs

    Repair nights for bikes, kettles, lamps and jackets.

  • River

    Clean-ups along the Clyde, the Kelvin and the canal.

One Saturday at the Govan larder

Eight neighbours, two and a half hours, 140 bags. This is what a morning of spare hours looks like from the inside.

  1. 09:40

    Keys, kettle, crates

    The wholesaler drops eleven crates at the side door. Whoever arrives first puts the kettle on.

  2. 10:00

    The crew arrives

    Eight people, four of them new. Everyone has a job and a name badge within five minutes.

  3. 10:20

    Sorting what came in

    Soft fruit first, tins last. Anything past its best goes to the soup pot.

  4. 10:45

    Bags packed, names on

    140 bags, each with a name and a note about allergies, lined up by the door.

  5. 11:30

    Lunch for whoever stayed

    The soup pot feeds the crew and anyone who came in out of the rain.

  6. 12:10

    The rest goes up the road

    Leftovers walk two streets to the community fridge, and the keys go back to Margaret.

Questions before your first hour

Anything else, ask us on 0141 000 0000. A person answers.

Do I need any experience?

No. Every group explains the job at the door, and most hours are things you already do at home: sorting, reading, pouring, weeding.

What if I can only come once?

Then come once. About a third of our volunteers give one hour a month or less, and groups plan their rotas around that.

Is there an age limit?

From 16 on your own, from 12 with an adult who signs up too. Reading in schools is for over-18s only.

Do I need a PVG check?

Only for hours with children or vulnerable adults. We arrange and pay for it, and it usually takes about two weeks.

Can my workplace come as a team?

Yes. Teams of up to twelve can take over a larder shift or a garden day. Write to us a month ahead.

Where does my money go?

82p of every pound pays for matching, checks and group supplies. The rest keeps the lights on at the office.
